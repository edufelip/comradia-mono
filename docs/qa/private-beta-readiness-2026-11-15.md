## Minimum bounded beta protocol

Run only after the blocking legal/support and invite-access gates are resolved. Use a small owner-selected invite-only cohort, a separate synthetic test account for destructive checks, and the dev project/build explicitly identified to participants. No participant invitations are authorized by this report.

1. **Release candidate identity:** record source SHA, signed Android APK/AAB and iOS archive/TestFlight build numbers/hashes, Firebase app IDs/variants, and minimum OS/device matrix. Confirm correct dev-vs-prod Firebase config and App Check attestation. Keep crash report receipt tied to that exact binary.
2. **Access/onboarding:** verify the invite gate with valid, invalid, expired, and reused invite cases; verify age-18+ restriction, consent version, locale, terms/privacy links, support contact and account deletion entry point before admitting a participant.
3. **Safety and moderation:** on synthetic accounts, report profile and chat with each supported reason, optional details, block side effect, persisted report visibility, owner push receipt while app foreground/background, and duplicate/retry behavior. Verify alert text contains no identity/free-text. Verify owner can find the report in Firestore console and follow documented manual triage. Exercise underage escalation only with synthetic data and confirm owner procedure before any real case.
4. **Deletion:** on a synthetic account only, confirm reauthentication/confirmation, account deletion, profile/photo/likes/passes/matches/blocks/push-token cleanup, sent-message cleanup and expected retained report. Never use an owner account.
5. **Notifications and recovery:** native iOS and Android, each with two synthetic accounts, test opt-in, registration, match/message receipt foreground/background/terminated, tap routing, blocked and signed-out safety, token refresh/revocation, and permission denial. Record provider acceptance separately from visible device receipt.
6. **Crash reporting:** on each exact beta binary, cause one deliberate synthetic nonfatal/crash under controlled QA, confirm the event appears in the correct Firebase Crashlytics app with build/version metadata, and confirm the app recovers/relaunches. No real user data in breadcrumbs/custom keys.
7. **Support and stop criteria:** confirm a monitored contact and named on-call owner during the beta window. Stop admissions for any under-18 account, report-routing failure, account-deletion failure, sensitive-data exposure, unexpected external push, crash loop, or broken legal/support route. Triage reports manually; the system does not auto-remove accounts and report pushes are not retried after failure.
