## 5. Sensitive data and your consent

The LGPD treats information about political opinion, union membership, and sex life or sexual orientation as **sensitive data**. In Comradia, this includes:

- your political values and causes;
- your union membership, if you enter it;
- "who you are looking for", because it can reveal your sexual orientation.

We use this data **only with your specific, highlighted consent** (art. 11, I of the LGPD). You give this consent at sign-up, when you accept the consent text, version 2026-11-13.

Important points:

- Without this data, the app cannot build your profile or show you people. So to use Comradia, you need to consent.
- You can withdraw your consent at any time. To do so, **delete your account in the app** (section 10). Deleting the account ends your use of the service.
- If we change what this data is used for, we will ask for new consent first.

## 6. Who processes your data

We use Google services to run Comradia. Google Cloud and Firebase process the data on our behalf, as **operators**:

- **Firebase Authentication:** sign-in by phone (SMS code), Google and Apple.
- **Cloud Firestore:** profiles, likes, matches, messages, blocks and reports.
- **Cloud Storage:** your profile photo.
- **Cloud Functions:** sends the match and message alerts.
- **Firebase Cloud Messaging:** delivers alerts to your phone. On iPhone, delivery goes through Apple's servers (APNs).
- **Firebase Crashlytics (may be used):** reports app crashes so we can fix bugs.
- **Firebase App Check (may be used):** helps confirm that requests come from our app.

If you sign in with Apple or Google, those providers also process your sign-in data under their own policies.

**We do not sell data.** **We have no ads.** **We do not use third-party analytics tools** to track you.

## 8. Alerts (notifications)

- Alerts do not show names, message text, profile details or political values.
- The lock-screen alert only says that there is a new match or a new message.
- You ask for notifications when you turn them on in your profile. You can turn them off in your phone's settings.

## 9. Sharing

We do not share your data with advertising companies. We share data only:

- with the operators listed in section 6, so the app works;
- with other people in the app, only what appears on your public profile and in your conversations;
- with authorities, when the law requires it or when someone faces a serious risk.

Your public profile does **not** show your birth date, phone number or email.

## 10. How long we keep data

- **While your account exists:** we keep the data so the app works.
- **When you delete your account in the app:** we delete your profile, your photo, your likes, your passes, your matches, your blocks and your notification tokens. We also delete the messages you sent. A cleanup routine may delete messages the other person sent inside a match that no longer exists.
- **Reports:** we keep reports for as long as needed to review the case and protect other people, even after the account is deleted. [NOTE FOR REVIEWER: set the retention period.]

## 11. Security

- Database and storage security rules limit who can read and write each piece of data.
- Your birth date can only be read by your own account.
- Only you can change or delete your photo.
- Data travels encrypted between the app and the servers.
- No system is 100% secure. If an incident could cause a relevant risk to you, we will tell you and the ANPD, as the LGPD requires.

## 12. Minors (under 18)

- Comradia is **only for people 18 or older**.
- We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18.
- If we learn, or receive a report, that a person is under 18, **we remove the account**.

1. confirmation that we process your data;
2. access to your data;
3. correction of incomplete, inaccurate or outdated data;
4. anonymization, blocking or deletion of unnecessary data or data processed unlawfully;
5. portability of your data to another provider;
6. deletion of data processed with your consent;
7. information about who we share your data with;
8. information about the option to refuse consent and its consequences;
9. withdrawal of consent.

**To delete your account:** in the app, go to **Profile** and tap **Delete account**.

You can also file a complaint with Brazil's **National Data Protection Authority (ANPD)**.
